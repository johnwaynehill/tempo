10 · Iconography

No icon library. Every glyph is a hand-authored SVG at 24×24 with a 1.75px stroke — thick enough to read at a glance, thin enough to disappear into a quiet UI.

12 · Principles

The rules we hold.

Five commitments that shape every decision.

01

No-line rule

Boundaries come from background hierarchy — not 1px borders. The six-step surface scale does the heavy lifting. Exceptions: form-field underlines, markdown separators.

02

No drop shadows

On ambient UI, shadows are replaced by colour shifts. Only floating modals earn them: 16–32px blur, 6% opacity, nothing more.

03

Warm over cool

Even neutrals lean warm. Pure greys feel clinical; pure blacks feel punitive. ADHD brains already have enough cold input.

04

Whitespace is a feature

Density is hostile. 1.6× line-height, generous margins, one primary action per screen. The interface should breathe at the pace you do.

05

Gentle feedback only

No confetti cannons. No streak-shame red. Completion earns a small sparkle; missing a day earns silence. The app never punishes you.
